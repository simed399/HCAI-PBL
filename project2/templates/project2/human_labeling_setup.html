How Human Labeling Works

Step 1: The system selects the most informative samples using your chosen strategy.
Step 2: You manually label each sample as positive or negative sentiment.
Step 3: The model retrains on your labels and improves its performance.
Step 4: The process repeats until stopping criteria are met.
